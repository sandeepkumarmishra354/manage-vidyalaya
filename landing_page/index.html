Students & Admissions

A complete profile for every student

Manage admissions end-to-end and keep every student's academic, family and attendance history in one searchable record.

  • Fast, searchable student directory with class & status filters
  • Rich profiles covering academics, guardians, attendance and fees
  • Guardian records with sibling linking and dedicated profile pages
Attendance

Mark attendance in seconds, not minutes

Daily attendance and calendar views with automatic working-day calculations that respect holidays and half-days — synced straight into payroll and reports.

  • One-click "mark all present / absent" for a whole class
  • Calendar & report views to spot patterns instantly
  • Feeds directly into staff payroll calculations
Fees & Billing

Billing that adapts to how your school actually charges

Create flexible fee structures — monthly, quarterly, annual or one-time — apply discounts and waivers, and track every rupee collected and outstanding.

  • Session-based or evergreen fee structures, with per-student overrides
  • Automatic invoice generation with receipts and payment history
  • Sibling discounts, waivers and an at-a-glance outstanding balance
Exams & Report Cards

From mark entry to printable report cards

Set up exams per class, capture marks, and publish report cards that are ready to print with signatures.

  • Track submission status across subjects and teachers
  • Support for back-papers and re-evaluations
  • Printable, signed report cards for every student
Library

Never lose track of a book again

Catalog every title, issue and return books in a click, and keep an eye on overdue copies before they become a problem.

  • Searchable catalog with ISBN, author and copy tracking
  • Issue / return workflow with automatic due dates
  • Overdue highlighting plus full history & reporting
Transport

Routes, stops and riders — organized

Plan routes with seat capacity, assign students to stops, and see who's riding which bus at a glance.

  • Route and stop management with live capacity tracking
  • One-click student-to-route assignment
  • Clear roster view for every vehicle
Staff & Payroll

HR and payroll, without the spreadsheets

Maintain a full staff directory with salary structures, then run monthly payroll with automatic attendance-based calculations and printable payslips.

  • Employee records with designation, department & login access
  • Configurable salary components — HRA, DA, PF and more
  • One-click payroll runs with signed, printable payslips
Houses & ID Cards

The finishing touches that make it feel complete

Run a house-points leaderboard to keep students motivated, and generate professional ID cards in a choice of templates — ready to print in bulk.

  • Real-time house leaderboard with point history
  • Four ready-made ID card templates
  • Bulk generation and printing by class
